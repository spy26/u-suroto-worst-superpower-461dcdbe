# Benchmark — Worst Superpower (u-suroto-worst-superpower-461dcdbe)

Competition: one line, max 140 characters, inventing the most useless superpower
imaginable. Judge: deadpan comedy editor, pairwise, criteria in order:
(1) genuinely useless, (2) uselessness lands as a joke you can picture,
(3) unexpected / fresh, (4) tight and specific with a concrete detail,
(5) clear on a single reading. Explicitly stale: slow flight, invisibility
while nobody watches. A power that is really a curse, or quietly helps, loses.

Feed sampling: attempted before writing variants — the competition page fetch
returned HTTP 404 and the feed API route does not exist, so no live sample of
the ~507 existing entries was available. Mitigation: avoided every known
cliche family named in the brief plus the standard templates (flight,
invisibility, time-stop, teleport, weather control, plant talk, gold touch)
except where a variant reworks one with a genuinely new limitation.

Scores are 1–5 on: F = funniness, U = uselessness of the power (higher =
more useless), R = fresh angle, C = clarity in one line. All variants are
within the 140-char limit (verified by character count).

## Notes per variant

- V1: strong, but the time-stop-with-a-catch template is a known joke family; loses freshness to V2.
- V2: mind-reading is a classic power, but the target (goldfish), the shared single thought (one pebble), and the deadpan kicker ("not even a nice pebble") are all specific, visual, and unexpected. The power achieves literally nothing.
- V3: teleport-to-the-same-spot is a well-worn gag; the "out of breath" tag is good but not enough to rescue freshness.
- V4: the "already spent, on a Tuesday in 2019" twist is very fresh and deadpan; slightly less picturable in use than V2, and "eleven minutes younger" is a weaker visual gag.
- V5: talking to plants is a classic; the weather complaint is a decent tag but the angle is saturated.
- V6: funny, but "five seconds into the future of a finished show" is slightly muddled on first reading (clarity 4).
- V7: good picture (indoor rain, one drop at a time), but summoning-rain-with-a-catch is a familiar template.
- V8: fridge-weather is a neat limitation, but weather-control-limited is a common template and the joke lands softer than V2.
- V9: turning already-gold things gold is useless, but the Tuesday condition is arbitrary rather than funny; weakest punchline.
- V10: clean and very useless; "after you have already said it" is a known paradox-joke shape.
- V11: fails criterion 1 hardest — healing paper cuts is mildly useful, so it is a weak power, not a useless one. Also the "in writing" condition adds clutter.
- V12: telekinesis that duplicates what hands do is a good paradox, but the shape is a known gag.

## Winner: V2

> I can read the minds of goldfish, but they are all thinking about the same pebble, and it is not even a nice pebble.

- 116 characters, 24 words — inside the 140-char gate with room to spare.
- Total 20/20: the only variant scoring 5 on all four axes.
- Rationale: it satisfies criterion 1 completely (the power yields zero usable information — one pebble, already known, not nice); criterion 2 (the reader pictures a hero solemnly eavesdropping on a goldfish's pebble thought); criterion 3 (goldfish mind-reading with a single shared pebble thought is not a familiar gag, unlike flight/invisibility/teleport variants); criterion 4 (three concrete details: goldfish, same pebble, not even nice); criterion 5 (one sentence, no setup needed).
- Runner-up: V1 (19) and V4 (19). V1 loses on freshness (time-stop family); V4 loses slightly on picturability.
